# Handover, 5 Sep 2026 (covers 2–4 Sep)

*Written by the session that built the scrims page, opponent bench, ban board,
team history and champion filter. Committed verbatim so the reasoning survives.*

## 1. Decisions and reasoning

- Substitutes: first built as a `sub` flag that kept the sub in the roster table under the starter. User found six rows too much, so subs moved to a one-line bench under the table and are excluded from the ban board. The "sub" tag on ban cards came and went with that.
- Ban board caps at two champions per player so one one-trick can't fill it. Cards toggle (second click removes) after the user asked; a disabled button was the first cut.
- "As a team" history counts a game when three or more of the five are on one side, and includes normal draft (400) because amateur five-stacks practise there. Their bans in those games were deliberately not built: it needs a new field on cached matches and therefore a `CACHE_VERSION` bump.
- Champion filter: started page-local on Analysis, then made global with `sessionStorage` so the name follows across pages. The browser `<datalist>` was tried and replaced with a custom typeahead because it can't be styled and lists all 173. The filter only bites once the text resolves to a champion, so half-typed "ka" doesn't empty pages. Hand-logged results and prep-only scrim groups step aside under a filter.
- Scouting depth: three pages of ids and 40 fetches per queue per run. Deeper paging was rejected as describing a different player. Roughly two minutes per player was accepted.
- Bomb theme made the default for new visitors. Stored theme choices win. The 128px and favicon crops are tighter than the login art because the source has wide padding.
- User chose not to send Riot a follow-up ticket or App Notes.
- Reverse fallback (a series reading a scrim opponent's record) was not built, pending the user.
