## 3. Decisions and reasoning

- **A separate reviewer account, not the site's own.** The reviewer account
  must have 2FA off (or Riot cannot sign in) and its password goes into a
  ticket several Riot staff can read; a site identity account must have 2FA
  on and will hold Firebase, registrar and portal mail. One account cannot be
  both. `bomsquad.test@gmail.com` is the **reviewer** account: recovery address
  removed (with the password alone anyone can read it, masked, and start a
  recovery flow), 2FA off, attached to nothing. A real site account, with 2FA,
  is to be made later when one is needed. The account is to be **disabled or
  rotated once the review lands**.
- **Role `viewer`**, granted on Admin › Access (`+ Add member` defaults to
  `role: 'viewer', active: true`). Confirmed signed in on `/home` with the
  viewer badge and no Edit mode pill. An editor account can be offered to
  Riot in the ticket if they want to drive a draft themselves.
- **Disclose the model provider in the ticket, in two or three sentences.**
  This is a different call from the App Notes: those ask Riot's permission on
  eligibility and invite a "no"; this is a material fact about how the
  product works that the reviewer will see in the new walkthrough regardless.
  Discovered is worse than disclosed, and the answer is already written —
  no puuid, email, token or raw payload leaves; the other team goes as
  champions in seats with no IDs, names, ranks or records; post-game only.
- **Do not volunteer the two App Notes questions** (production key for a
  non-public project; the collector as a second project). Hold them until
  Riot raises a concern. Unchanged from 2 Sep.
- **Do not restore email/password sign-in pre-emptively.** It was removed on
  9 Sep because nobody used it. It is the fallback if Riot reports a Google
  security challenge or a blocked popup: enable the provider in the Firebase
  console, create the user, add a small form to `login.component.html`,
  deploy; the `access/{email}` gate still applies on top. About an hour.
- **Re-record the walkthrough** at 1920×1080 (the root font scales with the
  window, so a 2560 screen renders ~70% larger), 5–8 minutes, mostly from the
  viewer account with one editor segment for the draft and the same on-screen
  note as last time. Shot list: sign-in as the viewer; Home; Roster and a
  sheet; Comps; Prep & Draft with the scouting view held on public ranked
  facts only; the draft room through a full sequence, the fearless burn on
  game 2, the advisor offering several options each with its game count;
  Games → a review → the film room; champion win rates by rank; the footer
  attribution.
